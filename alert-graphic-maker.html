How to use this page
  1. Pick your MW group in the box at the top of the page, so that the right logo and city are displayed.
  2. Choose the alert type and write what was seen in English. Tap “Translate the English with DeepL,” then paste the results into the Spanish box and read it over.
  3. Check the date, time, and place. The date and time default to right this very now. In the "Road, Business or Area" box, name a landmark people will recognize, like a gas station or an exit number.
  4. Take a map screenshot. In Google Maps, Apple Maps, or OpenStreetMap, search for the location and zoom until the main roads around it are on screen. Then screenshot: On PC or Linux, hit the PrtScn button on your keyboard. On iPhone press the side button and volume up together, on most Android phones, press Power and Volume Down.
  5. Add the map. Tap “Choose map image” and pick the screenshot. (*Note, this tool was designed by an anarchist who doesn't mind if you put any old image you like in the map box. Do you, little rebel. Do you.) After adding a map image, a red oval appears by default. Tap the spot on the preview to move it, or use the sliders to move, resize, stretch and rotate it. Make the oval long and rotate it to circle a street. If the search bar or buttons from Google Maps show, use the zoom and move sliders to frame them out.
  6. Add supplemental photos if you have them. They are optional. The first optional photo goes on the left and the second photo optional goes on the right. Use the sliders to move them up or down and make them larger or smaller.
  7. Tap “Make image.” Check the result, then save it to your phone and copy the caption to post with it.

Start a new report with the button at the bottom of the form. Your photos and text never leave your device, and city, state and footer settings are remembered for next time.
